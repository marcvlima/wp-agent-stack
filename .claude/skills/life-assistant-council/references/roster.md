# Life Assistant Council roster — L0 (always in context)

One line per seat, ≤15 tokens. 12 seats · no reserved seats.
The Moderator (strongest model) never holds a seat; the founder ratifies.

## Vision & knowledge of the person

1. **life-assistant-vision** — the companion thesis, horizons, ambient computing, dependence ethics
2. **personal-knowledge-memory** — life graph, episodic/semantic memory, consolidation, forgetting
3. **personalization-learning** — preference learning, per-user adaptation, getting smarter over time
4. **context-sensing-fusion** — calendar, location, sensors, screen, audio fused into situation

## Presence & action

5. **proactivity-interruptibility** — when to speak, working-memory modeling, calm technology
6. **multi-channel-continuity** — phone, watch, glasses, car, desktop, speaker, chat handoff
7. **task-execution-agency** — real actions, connectors, confirmation policy, reversibility
8. **professional-work-integration** — work tools, roles, corporate boundary, knowledge work support

## Trust, quality & platform

9. **privacy-trust-data-rights** — on-device vs cloud, consent, portability, LGPD/GDPR, ownership
10. **companion-ux-wellbeing** — persona, tone, boundaries, anti-sycophancy, healthy reliance
11. **longitudinal-evaluation** — months-long metrics, proactive precision/recall, in-the-wild success
12. **platform-ecosystem-economics** — OS gatekeepers, integrations, pricing bar, moats, distribution

## Boundary with sibling councils

- Assistant **engineering** (voice pipeline latency, serving, harness, edge tiers) →
  `ai-assistant-council`; this council decides *what the life assistant should be
  and become*, and hands implementation there.
- Product prioritisation and metrics of the business → `product-operations-council`.
- Brand/UI craft → `agency-brainstorm`; IA/taxonomy → `product-taxonomy-council`.
