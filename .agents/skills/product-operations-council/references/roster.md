# Product Operations Council roster — L0 (always in context)

One line per seat, ≤15 tokens. 12 seats · no reserved seats.
The Moderator (Chief Product Officer voice) never holds a seat; the founder ratifies.

## Strategy & demand

1. **product-strategy** — vision, bets, portfolio focus, outcome over output, kill criteria
2. **continuous-discovery** — opportunity trees, weekly interviews, assumption tests
3. **demand-jtbd** — jobs to be done, switching forces, desired outcomes, segmentation
4. **market-competitive-intel** — TAM, positioning, competitor teardowns, category timing

## Measurement & growth

5. **product-metrics-analytics** — north star, funnels, cohorts, instrumentation, data trust
6. **experimentation-causal** — A/B validity, power, guardrail metrics, twyman's law
7. **growth-loops-plg** — activation, retention, loops, virality, product-led motion
8. **pricing-monetization** — packaging, willingness to pay, expansion, discount discipline

## Operations & delivery

9. **product-operations-systems** — the ProductOps core: data, insights, rituals, tooling, scale
10. **delivery-flow-methodology** — dual-track, WIP, flow metrics, DORA, shaping, cadence
11. **portfolio-economics** — unit economics, cost of delay, ROI, discovery-driven planning
12. **agentic-prodops** — AI agents running product workflows, evals, human-in-the-loop governance

## Boundary with sibling councils

- **Naming, taxonomy, IA and navigation** of the product surface → `product-taxonomy-council`.
- **Brand, UI, copy and experience craft** → `agency-brainstorm`.
- **Technical architecture** → `architecture-council`; **AI/ML implementation** →
  `ai-ml-implementation-council`; **assistant product decisions** → `ai-assistant-council`.
- This council owns *what to build, for whom, why, measured how, and how the
  product organisation runs* — not how it is engineered or how it looks.
